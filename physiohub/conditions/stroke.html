Stroke / Hemiplegia

Category: Neurological

Definition

An acute neurological injury due to interruption of blood supply to part of the brain resulting in focal deficits.

Causes / Risk Factors

  • Ischemic stroke
  • Hemorrhagic stroke
  • Hypertension, diabetes, smoking

Clinical Features

  • Sudden unilateral weakness
  • Facial droop
  • Speech disturbances

Assessment

NIHSS, Motor exam, Sensory exam, Gait assessment, Fugl-Meyer.

Physiotherapy Goals

  • Improve mobility and safety
  • Increase independence in ADLs

Treatment / Rehabilitation Principles

Task-specific training, gait training, balance retraining, strengthening and neurofacilitation approaches as appropriate.

Exercise Examples

  • Sit-to-stand practice
  • Weight-shifting
  • Treadmill training (with harness if needed)

Precautions

Monitor blood pressure, signs of deterioration, cardiac precautions as indicated.
